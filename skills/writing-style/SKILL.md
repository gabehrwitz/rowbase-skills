# Writing Style

How to write any prose Gabe may reuse.

## Core Rule

Write closer to spoken language than formal written language.

Use words you would actually say out loud. If a sentence sounds like something
you'd only ever type, rewrite it until it sounds like something you could say
to a smart friend.

## Defaults

- Be direct.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs.
- Keep the argument visible.
- Cut throat-clearing.
- Strive to make the writing unsummarizable: leave so little fluff that removing
  words also removes interesting ideas.
- Do not inflate ordinary claims.
- Use technical language when it adds precision, not status.
- Let important ideas sound simple if they are simple.
- Use colons sparingly, and only when they are necessary.

## Drafting

1. Explain the idea plainly first.
2. Turn that explanation into the draft.
3. Read it mentally as speech.
4. Rewrite any sentence that sounds stiff, grandiose, or generic.

## Editing

When revising AI-generated or rough prose:

- Keep the strongest idea.
- Remove decorative phrasing.
- Replace abstract claims with specific stakes, examples, or consequences.
- Preserve useful sharpness, but avoid manifesto voice unless asked for it.
- Prefer "this matters because..." over dramatic reframes.

## Things To Avoid

- Corporate proposal filler.
- Fake urgency.
- "Not X, but Y" as a default move.
- Over-polished three-part lists.
- Words you would not say in conversation.
- Big claims that cannot be defended in the next sentence.

## Exceptions

Do not over-casualize legal, school, government, or sensitive family/admin
writing. Match the context, but still avoid stiffness where it adds nothing.
